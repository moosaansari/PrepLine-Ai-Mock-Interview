How it works

Three steps, no setup.

Pick a role, talk through the questions, get told what to fix.

Step 1

Tell it what you're interviewing for

Pick your target role, your years of experience, and whether you want technical, behavioral, or mixed questions.

Step 2

Answer out loud

Prepline asks a question and you answer by talking, not typing. It follows up the way a real interviewer would if your answer left something out.

Step 3

Get a plain breakdown

You see how you structured the answer, how much you rambled, and the specific lines to rewrite. Not just a score.

Result

Feedback you can act on, not a grade.

It follows up like a real interviewer would

If your answer skips something important, Prepline asks about it, the same way a hiring manager pushes on a weak spot instead of moving on politely.

Example follow-up
"Why did you choose that locking strategy over an optimistic concurrency approach?"
